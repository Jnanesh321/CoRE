About CoRE

CoRE (Community Research & Engagement) is a network of practitioners, researchers, and volunteers who design and run programs that strengthen communities. We run education initiatives, community-led research, and entrepreneurship support tailored to local needs.

Our mission

To empower communities through evidence-based programs that connect people, knowledge and resources.

Our approach

  • Co-design with communities
  • Actionable, applied research
  • Capacity building and skills training
